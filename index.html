LAUNCHPAD 2027

A week-long competition for student founders.

Get notified when applications open

Most competitions hand you the answer.

Typical case competitions give every team an existing company and a problem someone already solved. Launchpad does the opposite. Every team receives the same working product, and the business around it is entirely theirs to build.

Four questions decide everything:

  1. Who is the customer?
  2. How does the business make money?
  3. How does it reach customers?
  4. Why will it work?

At the end of the week, teams pitch their answers to judges and defend them. Since everyone starts from the same product, the choices a team makes are what set it apart.
